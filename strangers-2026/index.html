  • We don't know yet whether our race (Trail Wisconsin) is going to be canceled but wanted to give y'all as much info as possible for transparency.

  • If you/we book anything, best to do it refundable in case your/others' plans change.
  • Annoyingly, we still have 3–4 runner slots to fill for the race, so we're simultaneously balancing the need to recruit with the possibility of a cancellation. As always, we'll figure it out (or I will greedily run every leg that people don't want).

Whether the race is on or off, Gibbs and I plan to go to the Midwest for a Strangers weekend, and we would love if literally all of you joined. Wisconsin, Illinois, WHEREVER.

Even if it's not glamping and running a Ragnar, we will plan a stellar away weekend that involves custom swag, outdoor activities (running? kayaking? dancing in the moonlight?), calling Gibbs a praying mantis, eating too many snacks, finding excellent bakeries, and maybe even a séance to contact the spirits of former Ragnar teammates (Brian still not invited).

My personal nightmare is that this email causes a ripple effect in which you second-guess whether you even want to do the race. (I promise, you do.) And then Ragnar is like GAME ON! But you've pulled out and so the team is now composed of me, Gibbs, a few seedy drifters we pick up on the black market, and a raccoon named Delores. The goal is not to send shockwaves of panic. The goal here is to just share what's happening and then make a solid backup plan with consenting adults.

What we need from you

We still don't know anything concrete from Ragnar, but we definitely want to hear from you. Please reply to me/Gibbs and let us know where you fall:

  • A) IN no matter what
  • B) IN for the race only
  • C) Still have questions (send those!)

Once we have a better idea of where everyone stands, we can start to figure out a backup plan that won't be disruptive or last minute and WILL be epic and fun as hell. Because the truth is, we fucking love you guys, always want to see you, and KNOW we can make that happen.

We didn't throw this curve ball, but we still know how to drive in runs.

HAVE A GREAT WEDNESDAY, STRANGERS!
